# Carom

A paddle. A ball. An angle your opponent didn't see coming. Carom is a fast browser duel on a dark court, where two amber obstacles keep changing the geometry between you and the far goal.

The opening serve gives you a moment to get into position. Meet the ball squarely for a clean return, or catch it near your paddle's edge to send it climbing toward a wall. Move as you strike and the shot starts to curve: a little spin can turn a safe return into a difficult read.

The obstacles sway and rotate together. Their tilted faces offer bank shots that change from rally to rally, sending the ball through new gaps and back toward unexpected corners. Every paddle contact makes the pace a little faster, so long rallies reward both quick reactions and an eye for the next angle.

Take on the machine in Solo, or challenge a friend on the same keyboard in Versus. The first player to eleven wins, but you need a two-point lead. At ten-all, every return matters. Keep moving, find your bank, and make match point yours.
